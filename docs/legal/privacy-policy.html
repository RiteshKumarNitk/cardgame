SuitClash — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]  ·  Publisher: [PUBLISHER LEGAL NAME]

This policy explains what information the SuitClash mobile game ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. By installing and using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, coin balance, unlocked achievements, cosmetics, and settings are saved locally on your device. This data stays on the device unless cloud sync (below) is active, and is removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Erase my data.

2. Cloud sync (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore)

So your progress survives a reinstall or new device, the app signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random user ID that is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or Google account. Against that anonymous ID we store:

  • coin balance, total stars, number of levels completed, and the list of completed level IDs;
  • your best Daily Challenge score and, only if you set one, the display name you choose, which appears on the public leaderboard;
  • a "last synced" timestamp.

We do not store contacts, location, photos, messages, or device identifiers through cloud sync. You can erase this cloud data at any time from Settings → Erase my data, or by contacting us (section 7).

3. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played in aggregate (for example: levels started and completed, session length, feature usage). This includes an app-instance identifier and coarse technical data such as device model, operating-system version, language, and country. It is not used to identify you personally.

4. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report containing the error, a stack trace, and the device state at the time of the crash (model, OS version, memory, whether the app was in the foreground). This helps us fix bugs.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-app purchases (such as removing future ads or buying cosmetics) are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment; we never see or store your card or payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded so we can unlock the item.

6. Advertising

This version of the app does not show ads and does not collect an advertising ID. If ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated first and you will be asked for any consent the law requires.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is processed on our behalf by Google (Firebase / Google Play) as described above. It may be disclosed if required by law. Firebase processing is described in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

8. Data retention

Local data lasts until you clear it or uninstall. Cloud sync and leaderboard data are kept until you request deletion. Analytics data is retained per Google's default Firebase Analytics retention settings; Crashlytics reports are retained up to 90 days.

9. Children

SuitClash is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, or to request deletion of your cloud/leaderboard data, contact us at the address below. In the app you can also disable sound/music, reset local progress, restore purchases, and erase your data from Settings.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
